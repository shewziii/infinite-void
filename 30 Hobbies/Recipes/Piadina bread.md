## Makes: 6

## Ingredients:

2 cups flour
1/2 cup lukewarm water
2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
1 teaspoon salt

## Instructions:

Mix ingredients into a bowl until you get a rough dough. Knead dough until it's soft and smooth. Cut dough into 6 tight balls. Cover balls with a towel and let rest for 15-30 minutes. Cook them.
