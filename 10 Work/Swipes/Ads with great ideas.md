This ad could just as easily say, "Meta Ads course (Facebook Ads + Instagram Ads)," but that doesn't really separate it from the competition at all. The above ad uses the same idea and makes it a lot more interesting to the prospect. It appeals to the reader's emotional core.

![[courage ad.jpg]]

This ad has a great lead that doesn’t mention what it’s selling or the price of anything (a book for $5.95). The word “courage” gets the reader to feel something and look into themselves about potential reasons they might not be making that amount of money per year.
