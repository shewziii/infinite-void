## Fill out pre-writing sheet

The purpose of this worksheet is to get as much of the writing done as possible. Filling out a worksheet that asks you specific questions is a better mental framework than having to come up with everything on the fly. It gives structure, ensure you don't forget anything, and can help with mental blockage.

## Sequence and structure

Once the worksheet is filled out, it's time to figure out the sections you need and the order you're going to include them in. To do this, you should map out a wireframe, which outlines what each section will look like and how it will be structured.

A good structure for sections and headlines is as follows:

1.  Headline and a hook
	1. This tells them what the page is about and why they should be interested.
2. Who it's for 
	1. The problem your audience is facing. Describe the problem so they can see that they're in the right place.
3. Introduce the product
	1. Introduces the product and the solution to the problem they're facing. This can be brief. Explain how it works, what it includes, and why you included those things and how they'll help them
4. Social proof and testimonials
	1. This is just proof that the solution works 
5. FAQs
